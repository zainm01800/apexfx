- **Prop-firm reading:** funded accounts need the drawdown contained, so the Sharpe-optimal cap need not be the prop-optimal cap. On overall drawdown 6.5% IS the prop-optimal rung: grid-lowest maxDD (16.32%) and Calmar-optimal (0.5375). Caveat for a hard daily-loss rule: the worst single day is -5.09% at 6.5% vs -3.77% at 4.5% — a 5% daily-loss limit is only contained in-sample at the 4.5% rung.
