Frozen method

The rules below were fixed before the audit result was run.

1 · Signal

Positive momentum only

At each calendar month-end close, rank ETFs by 252-session return divided by 63-session log-return volatility. Non-positive momentum stays in cash.

2 · Portfolio

Three equal-weight slots

Hold up to three ETFs at 95% gross exposure. Only one ETF can be selected from each predeclared economic cluster.

3 · Execution

Next session open

The month-end decision is never filled on the same close. Rebalances occur at the next common ETF-market open with transaction costs applied.

4 · Boundaries

Long-only control

No shorts, leverage, intrabar stop or target assumptions. A final liquidation cost is included rather than assuming a free exit.

What this result does not establish

These limitations are part of the audit, not small print.

  • Not blindThe historical cache was already accessible before the test, so causal replay cannot be called a true blind backtest.
  • Not total returnCached ETF bars are price returns. Dividends and cash interest were not reconstructed.
  • Not comparable to A/B/CR is a USD ETF control. A/B/C are separate GBP-labelled forward-paper books with different rules and accounting.
