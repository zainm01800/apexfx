Method, limitations & promotion requirements

What is frozen

Lagged VIX selects between oversold long-only ETF entries and sector reversal long/short baskets. Stops are fixed at 1.5 × ATR20 with a five-session holding clock. Each new book starts from zero forward profit with its own GBP cash ledger.

What is not proven

Daily adjusted ETF bars are a proxy for CFD execution. Intraday sequence, actual bid/ask spread, contract sizes, firm-specific reset time and payout rules are not a funded-account certification. A historical maximum drawdown is not a bound on the next loss.

Forward assessment

Observe new trades after activation, reconcile data and fees, preserve missed-run evidence and measure risk-limit incidents. No automatic graduation at an arbitrary profit percentage or number of days.

Archived studies

Existing books and ledgers are retained. Their currencies, start dates and strategy rules differ, so a higher raw balance is not proof of a better engine.
